# 8 Bit Mammoth — Group Configurations

Sizing options offered to venues. The skill should ask which configuration is booked and shape copy accordingly.

## Small — 5 to 8 piece

- **Default instrumentation:** four-piece rhythm section + four-piece horn section.
- **Scaling:** rhythm can drop to 3, horns can drop to 2. Bare minimum 5.
- **Repertoire focus:** video-game material exclusively, or near-exclusively. The Cowboy Bebop charts and the large-production anime arrangements are not in scope at this size.
- **Vocalist:** optional. Most small bookings are instrumental.
- **Vibe to convey:** combo. Tight, punchy, conversational. Easier to fit on smaller stages and into tighter rooms.

## Mid — 10 piece

- **Instrumentation:** expanded rhythm and horns relative to the small group.
- **Repertoire focus:** primarily video-game music, with more harmonic and textural depth than the small group. Some larger anime/TV material opens up.
- **Vocalist:** optional.
- **Vibe to convey:** ensemble. More arrangement detail, fuller voicings, larger dynamic range.

## Large — 12+ piece (full production)

- **Instrumentation:** full ensemble, the largest configuration the band offers.
- **Repertoire focus:** the whole book is available, including the Cowboy Bebop / Seatbelts arrangements and the larger-production anime material.
- **Vocalist:** optional in general, but the Bebop vocal book specifically requires the singer.
- **Vibe to convey:** big-band-scaled production. Cinematic. Designed for rooms and bills that can carry the size.

## Choosing the right framing

- If the booking is a small bar / cocktail set / corporate background: small group, talk in combo terms.
- If the booking is a headlining show at a music venue with the full production: large group, you can reference Bebop and the cinematic side.
- If unspecified, ask — the same band sounds different at different sizes and the copy should reflect what's actually showing up that night.

## What never changes across sizes

The musicianship and the seriousness about the source material. A 5-piece reading game music is not a "lite" version — it's a different arrangement of the same identity.
