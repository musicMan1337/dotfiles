# 8 Bit Mammoth — Band Overview

The factual core. Other skills read this to ground any copy in real specifics.

## What we are

A horn-driven instrumental ensemble that arranges video game, anime, and TV music in funk and jazz idioms, with occasional excursions into other genres. Scope ranges from small combo to big-band-sized productions.

## Stylistic identity

- **Primary languages:** funk and jazz. Think tight horn writing, deep pocket, room for improvisation.
- **Default tempo/feel:** uptempo grooves, swing, funk, fusion-adjacent. **Ballads are rare and should not be reached for as a default style descriptor.** When describing what the band does to source material, lean on "grooves," "horn arrangements," "rebuilt as funk/jazz," "room to solo" — not "ballads," not "delicate," not "slow burn."
- **Source material:** game/anime/TV soundtracks reimagined as live arrangements — not novelty covers, not chiptune.
- **Reading depth:** the music is taken seriously. But in promo copy, *don't say* "the charts are real / the solos are live / we mean it." Saying it out loud reads as defensive. Let the description of what we play imply the seriousness.

## Repertoire snapshot

See [repertoire.md](repertoire.md) for the full library. High-level:
- The bulk is video-game music spanning Nintendo, Sega, Rare, Square, Blizzard.
- Larger configurations carry full Cowboy Bebop / Seatbelts arrangements, including vocal charts.
- A handful of TV / film entries (Hey Arnold!, Star Wars).

## Group configurations

See [configurations.md](configurations.md) for sizing and instrumentation. Quick map:
- **Small (5–8 piece):** rhythm + horn quartet, scales rhythm or horns down to a trio/duo if needed. Video-game-focused book.
- **Mid (10 piece):** fuller rhythm and horns, opens up more material.
- **Large (12+ piece):** full production, including the Cowboy Bebop book and other big-production anime arrangements.
- **Vocalist:** optional add-on at any size; mandatory for the Bebop vocal charts.
