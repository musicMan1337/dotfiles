# Palworld 1.0 — Gear, Accessories, Weapons, Spheres & Tech

1.0 launched July 10, 2026 (build 1.100.427). Built from first-days-post-launch guides; EA-era conflicts flagged. Two clearly-fabricated wiki entries ("Meowmere," "V1 Armor (ULTRAKILL)") and cross-game contamination ("Terraprisma," "Overheat Rifle") were excluded. A creator video repeats the "V1 Armor" claim (a ~780 Dog-Coin legendary armor from a medal merchant, "better than 95% of craftable armors"); do not treat it as real without in-game confirmation.

## Level cap & accessory-slot change

- **Player/Pal level cap 80** in 1.0 (50 → 55 → 60 → 65 → 80).
- **Accessory slots: 2 base, expandable to 4** in 1.0 (EA capped at 2). 1.0 also blocks equipping two of the same-named accessory.

Recommended 4-slot loadouts:
1. **Bossing:** Dogen Emblem + Attack Pendant + element-matched Emperor's Baton/Ring + Phantom Ring.
2. **Survivability:** Warsect Terra's Belt + Silvegis Emblem + Defense Pendant + Life Pendant (or Vanguard Charm if biome-hopping).
3. **Exploration:** Ring of Freight (or Islander's Tool Belt) + Triple Jump Boots + Air Dash Boots + a biome undershirt.

Priority (creator guide): environmental Heat/Cold damage is a bigger early killer than combat, so craft/buy a climate Undershirt before spending Dog Coins on the 3rd/4th accessory slot.

Best pieces come from Alpha boss drops, dungeon chests, and Sakurajima/Feybreak region drops.

### Where the schematics are (creator guide)

Each is a walled tower with an unlock minigame (break ceiling stones / power a grid / connect a line), schematic inside, then craft. Epic-tier pieces (Phantom Ring, Islander's Tool Belt, Super Multiclimate) craft at a **Production Assembly Line**, one tier above the HQ Workbench used for the rest.

- **Multiclimate Undershirt** (Heat3 + Cold3): tower in the NE desert (area ~Lv40). Craft (HQ Workbench): 40 HQ Cloth, 20 Flame Organ, 20 Ice Organ, 20 Polymer. CONFLICT: the table lists a ~30k Gold merchant / Bellanoir drop instead; both a schematic-tower and a merchant source may exist, verify.
- **Attack Pendant** (Atk +3): near the same desert cluster (area ~Lv55). Craft: 25 Ingots, 20 Horns, 25 Palium Fragments, 2 Ancient Civilization Parts.
- **Defense Pendant** (Def +3): volcanic-island beach tower. Craft: 30 Ingots, 20 Ore, 25 Palium Fragments, 2 Ancient Civilization Parts.
- **Phantom Ring** (Epic): NE desert / icy island tower. Craft (Production Assembly Line): 30 Refined Ingots, ~10 Plastic, 15 Power Metal Ingots, 30 Ancient Bones.
- **Islander's Tool Belt** (Epic; carry +4, work speed +4): hidden mineshaft cave near an icy NW island (reach via the non-obvious NW path); guarded by a ~Lv35 pal. Craft (Production Assembly Line): 20 Refined Ingots, 40 Leather, 35 Palium Fragments, 30 Ancient Bark.
- **Growth Acceleration Belt** (Pal EXP +3): tower at the summit of a central-map mountain, easy minigame. Craft (HQ Workbench): 25 Ingots, 30 Palium Fragments, 10 Mysterious Mushrooms, 3 Ancient Civilization Parts. Cheap; get early to speed leveling of the wearer's active pals.
- **Super Multiclimate accessory** (Epic; Heat3 + Cold3 + HP+4): SE-island tower (near a "Scorched Ashland" waypoint). Craft (Production Assembly Line): 30 Polymer, 30 Bone, 20 Flame Organ, 20 Ice Organ, 30 Ancient Bones. Late-game slot-efficient replacement for the base Undershirt.

Heat/Cold/Lightweight variants exist at Pelt tier and up (Flame Organ for heat, Cryogenic Coolant/Ice Organ for cold). **Heat resist** for desert/volcano (Mount Obsidian, Desiccated Desert); **cold resist** for snow (Astral Mountains). Carry both, swap by destination.

Craft-at-level: ~4 Cloth, 12 Pelt, 23 Metal, 37 Refined, 46 Pal Metal, 55+ Plasteel, 68+ Ancient (once Soralite/Sunreach access opens).

Endgame: Legendary Plasteel needs 25 Ancient Civilization Parts (schematic from Oil Rig gold chests). Legendary Ancient Armor tops out 1,040 Def / 3,120 HP.

## Pal Gear / traversal tech

Pal Gear = equipment worn by specific pals (saddles in [mounts.md](mounts.md), plus harnesses/utility). Unlock: catch the pal → spend tech points → build at a **Pal Gear Workbench** (tech Lv6).

- **Foxparks's Harness** (tech 6, catch Foxparks): hold + swing as a handheld flamethrower (3 Leather, 5 Flame Organ, 5 Paldium). Ice variant: Foxparks Cryst's Harness. The pattern generalizes across elemental pals.

Grappling Gun tiers (Ancient Tech):

| Tier | Tech Lv | AT | Range | Cooldown |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Grappling Gun | 12 | 1 | 5 | 12s |
| Mega | 17 | 2 | 6 | 10s |
| Giga | 31–32 | 3 | 6.8 | 8s |
| Hyper | 47 (or 50) | 4 | 7.5 | 6s |

Gliders: 4 tiers; Giga Glider tech 40, Hyper Glider (top) tech 52; the new **Wing Pack** (pal-free glider, burns Wing Cells) unlocks at tech 80. Other QoL Ancient Tech: Feed Bags (auto-feed), Hip Lantern (hands-free light), Dimensional Pal Storage, Homeward Thundercloud (teleport home, level 22 or 30 disputed), Ore Mining Site I/II (auto ore).

## Tech-tree priorities

**Early (1–20):** Pal Sphere → Mega Sphere; core base/crafting stations; an early mount saddle; **Egg Incubator** (Ancient, ~Lv10, highest-priority Ancient unlock, gates breeding); Grappling Gun (Lv12); Hot Spring/Feed Box.

**Mid (20–50):** **Pal Essence Condenser** (Ancient, Lv14); Statue of Power; weapons toward Assault Rifle (45) + Pump Shotgun (43); armor Metal (23) → Refined (37); Production Assembly Line + Power Generator; Ability Glasses, Ring of Mercy, Mega Grappling Gun.

**Late/endgame (50–80):** Pal Metal (46) → Plasteel → Ancient armor (68+); Rocket Launcher (65) + Soralite/Paloxite weapons (67–80); save AT points for the expensive tier: Triple Jump Boots (70, 6 AT), Soralite Quarry (72, 7 AT), **Ancient Hatchery (76, 8 AT)**, Wing Pack (80, 9 AT); Ultra (35) + Legendary (44) Spheres as standard catch tools.

Heuristic: before spending an Ancient Tech Point, ask "does this save time on everything I do next?" Spheres, saddles, Incubator, Hot Spring, automation pay for themselves; decorative/situational unlocks go last.

## Best "power" items to rush

1. **Egg Incubator** (gateway to breeding/passive power).
2. **Mega → Giga Sphere** (gates which pals you keep).
3. **Grappling Gun** tiers (cheapest mobility-to-cost).
4. **Pal Essence Condenser + Statue of Power** habit (permanent compounding).
5. **Assault Rifle (45) → Rocket Launcher (65)** (content breakpoints).
6. **Warsect Terra's Belt / Dogen / Silvegis Emblem** (survive → tank/no-hit bosses).
7. **Ancient Series Armor** (68+, top defensive tier).
8. **Legendary energy weapons via relic recycling** (Beam Scatter/Plasma/Beam Launcher/Drone).
9. **Triple Jump + Air Dash Boots** (cheap traversal upgrade).
